## Test how candidates handle missing information

These exercises are an employer-created interview aid, not an official language test. Give every candidate the same instructions and preparation time. Score whether they confirm facts and seek help appropriately, rather than how quickly they speak.

## Try five short situations

| Scenario | Behaviour to observe |
| --- | --- |
| Reservation name differs | Checks details politely without guessing |
| Guest asks to change dates | Confirms the request and checks availability |
| Directions to breakfast | Gives clear information and checks understanding |
| Complaint about a room | Listens, summarizes and contacts the responsible person |
| Question about food ingredients | Checks with authorized staff instead of making assurances |

1. Score understanding, clarification, explanation and escalation from 0 to 2.
2. Write one observed example for each score.
3. Review differences between interviewers before deciding.

## Can this replace JLPT or an SSW examination?

No. Check official requirements separately. Use the results to choose follow-up questions and plan training. A candidate who asks for clarification may handle unfamiliar situations more safely than one who answers confidently without checking.
