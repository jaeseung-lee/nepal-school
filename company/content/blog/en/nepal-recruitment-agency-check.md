## Search the official record yourself

DoFE's agency search accepts a permission number or agency name and includes status information. A photograph of a certificate or a social-media profile is not a current registry check. Ask the agency for its exact legal name and permission number.

## Make a short verification record

1. Open the official DoFE agency search independently.
2. Search by permission number, then compare the legal name.
3. Check the displayed status and investigate discrepancies.
4. Compare the agency with the entity named on the contract and receipts.
5. Keep the search date and the questions still awaiting confirmation.

| If you find… | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Matching name and number | Continue checking the actual job and conditions |
| Different names | Ask for a documented explanation |
| Unclear or missing status | Confirm with DoFE before proceeding |

## Does a licence verify the job offer too?

No. Check the relevant job and approval records separately. Training institutes and consultants should not be assumed to hold recruitment authority. DoFE guidance also distinguishes institutional and individual routes; confirm the route for the specific destination rather than treating this agency check as the whole departure procedure.
