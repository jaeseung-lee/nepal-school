## Match the offer to the documents you will travel with

DoFE advises workers to understand their job and contract and obtain the required labour approval. Compare the employer, destination, duties and pay across the offer, contract, visa and approval record. If they disagree, resolve the discrepancy before departure.

## Prepare a personal departure folder

| Item | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Contract | Employer, duties, pay, hours, accommodation and term |
| Approvals | Correct identity, destination and employment details |
| Required preparation | Applicable orientation, medical and insurance records |
| Payments | Amount, purpose, recipient and receipt |
| Contacts | Employer, relevant agency and official assistance channels |

1. Read an explanation in a language you understand.
2. Obtain a complete signed contract and keep copies securely.
3. Check approval through the official service applicable to your route.
4. Leave copies and emergency contacts with a trusted person.

## Should you accept a different contract at the airport?

Do not sign terms you do not understand or that conflict with the agreed offer. Ask for an explanation and official assistance where needed. Fees and procedures can change; confirm the current requirements instead of relying on an old screenshot or another worker's case.
