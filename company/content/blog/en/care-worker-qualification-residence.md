## Treat qualification and immigration as separate milestones

Japan's “Nursing Care” residence status is for qualifying activities performed by people holding the certified care worker qualification under a contract with a Japanese organization. Official guidance also covers people qualifying through practical experience. Passing an examination alone does not automatically change residence status.

## Make an individual preparation file

| Milestone | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Examination eligibility | Applicable route and current requirements |
| Work and training history | Evidence issued by the relevant institutions |
| Qualification | Result, registration procedure and documents |
| Immigration application | Contract, current status and required evidence |

1. Check the candidate's route with the examination body's current guidance.
2. Agree who supports study time and document requests.
3. Track examination and immigration deadlines separately.
4. Confirm the next procedure after results are issued.

## Can employers promise long-term residence?

An employer can describe its study support and proposed role. It cannot promise an immigration decision. Do not present the general SSW No. 2 route as the care career route; check the care-specific qualification and residence procedures. Build the schedule around verified individual evidence.
