## Set training goals before the first shift

This 90-day plan is an operational example, not a statutory timetable. Adjust it to the person's experience and the facility's procedures. Passing a recruitment interview does not establish that someone can perform every task independently.

## Plan observation, practice and review

| Period | Suggested focus | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–7 | Workplace rules, reporting, emergency contacts | Employee explains the reporting route |
| Days 8–30 | Observe and practise assigned tasks with a trainer | Trainer records observed performance |
| Days 31–60 | Handover language and tasks needing improvement | Short weekly feedback notes |
| Days 61–90 | Review permitted duties and further training | Agreed next goals and supervisor sign-off |

1. Name a primary trainer and a backup.
2. Use the same checklist across shifts.
3. Ask the employee to explain instructions in their own words.
4. Record what still requires supervision and set a review date.

## Is day 90 an automatic independence deadline?

No. Progress depends on demonstrated ability and workplace requirements. Keep care techniques under qualified supervision. Share language-learning needs with support staff, while the facility remains responsible for its workplace training decisions.
