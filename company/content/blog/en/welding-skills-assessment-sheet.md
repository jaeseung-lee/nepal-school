## Specify what the employer actually needs

“Experienced welder” can describe very different work. Record the process, material, joint, position and drawing-reading requirements before arranging an assessment. This is a proposed hiring worksheet; it is not a qualification standard or proof of eligibility for an E-7 visa.

## Use one record for all candidates

| Area | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- |
| Task specification | Employer's required process and test conditions |
| Preparation | Drawing interpretation and equipment checks |
| Safety | Qualified assessor's observations and stop conditions |
| Execution | Work sequence and handling of instructions |
| Result | Inspection method, findings and limits of the assessment |

1. Have a qualified specialist define and supervise the test.
2. Explain instructions and safety rules in an understood language.
3. Stop the assessment if required safeguards are missing.
4. Retain the assessor's signed findings with candidate consent.

## Does a successful test guarantee placement?

No. The employer must separately verify experience, the offered job and immigration eligibility. Do not invent a national pass mark or present a sample worksheet as an official certificate. Ministry of Employment and Labour materials can support safety preparation, while the technical acceptance criteria must come from the applicable job and qualified assessor.
