## Compare the same conditions across every document

EPS uses a standard employment contract in the hiring process. Compare the offer, contract, translated explanation and accommodation information before the worker agrees. A monthly salary figure alone does not explain take-home pay or living costs.

## Review the terms together

| Item | Clarify before signing |
| --- | --- |
| Pay | Base pay, allowances, payment date and calculation basis |
| Work | Duties, workplace, hours, breaks and rest days |
| Housing | Address or type, occupancy and facilities |
| Costs | Accommodation, meals, utilities and the payment method |
| Changes | Contact person and written procedure for amendments |

1. Mark conflicting terms and obtain a corrected written explanation.
2. Check applicable law and current official guidance before making deductions.
3. Give both parties a copy of the final agreed documents.
4. Reconcile the first pay statement with the explanation given.

## Is a worker's signature enough to justify any housing charge?

No. Agreement alone does not settle compliance with wage and accommodation rules. Verify the applicable requirements for the workplace. Avoid applying a local guideline's amount as a nationwide fixed fee. Keep receipts and explanations understandable and accessible.
