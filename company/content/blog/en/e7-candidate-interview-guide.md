## On the E-7 route, the interview and the review look at the same things

> E-7 is not a channel where an employment centre refers candidates to you. The employer finds and chooses the person. Whatever the interview misses comes back as a problem at the review stage.

E-7 specified activities is a status reviewed against occupation-specific qualifications and experience. The certificates of career and degree a candidate submits are not internal reference material; they are the documents under review. Four things are worth verifying in the interview.

1. Use questions whose answers cannot be predicted to test real conversation.
2. Check that the candidate can explain their own resume and career certificate.
3. Give priority to experience in the same occupation you are hiring for.
4. Check how much the candidate paid to arrive, and under what headings.

## 1. Predictable questions give you nothing to judge

"Why do you want to work in Korea?" and "What are your strengths and weaknesses?" mostly return prepared answers. Applicants rehearse model responses to exactly these while preparing to leave. A smooth answer tells you nothing about whether they can follow instructions on the floor.

Move the subject matter to the candidate's own experience and ask so that the shape of the answer cannot be set in advance.

- Describe a situation that has no single correct answer.
- Take one step further into whatever the candidate has just said.
- Watch what happens when they do not understand you.

The ability the workplace needs is not a perfect sentence but the habit of checking again. Nodding past what was not understood turns into safety and quality problems after placement.

## 2. The resume and career certificate must be explainable by the candidate

Resumes are sometimes written by someone else, and a ghost-written document is often the tidier one, so paper alone will not separate them. On the E-7 route this is more than an interview risk. Because experience and qualifications are what gets reviewed, a resume the candidate cannot explain becomes a live risk at the application stage after you hire.

The check is simple. Pick one entry and ask about it on the spot.

- Ask how a day was ordered at the workplace they listed.
- Have them explain the equipment and terms they claim to have used.
- Re-confirm the dates and when their duties changed.

If it happened to them, the sequence and the details hold together even when the language is rough. If the writing is polished but the content cannot be explained, work through the remaining entries the same way.

## 3. Give priority to same-occupation experience

E-7 is assessed by occupation against experience and qualifications, so favouring candidates who have done the same work is not merely convenient; it runs with the grain of the programme. The Ministry of Justice's points-based route for skilled workers (E-7-4) requires at least five years of proper employment activity in Korea within the last ten years under the non-professional employment (E-9), vessel crew (E-10) or working visit (H-2) statuses, and excludes those with criminal records, unpaid taxes or immigration violations. Experience and a clean record of stay are themselves what is being assessed.

Someone applying only to earn money abroad and someone who did the work first and then prepared language and qualifications are starting from different places. The latter applied knowing what the job involves, so the gap between expectation and reality is smaller, and they are more likely to stay and settle.

- Whether the duties actually overlap with the occupation you are filling
- Whether language and qualifications were prepared on the back of that experience
- Whether the candidate can explain their reasons for leaving

## 4. Check what the candidate paid to arrive, and under what headings

Because E-7 hiring happens directly, without a public referral process, there is room for private intermediaries between the candidate and the employer. Establish during the interview how much the candidate paid before arrival and under what headings.

A person who arrives carrying costs they cannot service may leave the workplace or take on work they are not permitted to do in order to repay them. Since E-7 wage requirements are set each year by government notice, reading that year's notice alongside what the candidate has paid makes the judgement easier. If the amount is out of proportion to expected wages, or the candidate cannot account for the headings, treat it as a signal to re-examine the recruitment channel rather than the individual.

## Comparing interview question types

| What you want to see | Rehearsable question | Question that cannot be rehearsed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Communication | Is your Korean good? | Tell me which part of what I just said was unclear. |
| Document accuracy | How many years of experience? | Walk me through a day in the job on your certificate. |
| Occupational fit | Can you do this work? | What went wrong most often, and what did you do about it? |
| Cost burden | Did you pay a fee? | Explain what you have paid so far and what each item was for. |

## How we select candidates

Jeongwoo Human Resource Development Institute applies exactly these criteria when selecting students. Instead of questions with settled answers, we check whether a conversation can be sustained from the candidate's own experience, and we verify in person that the candidate can explain what their resume and career documents say.

We give priority to applicants who hold experience in the same occupation and prepared their language and qualifications on the back of it, and we confirm and record the headings and amounts each candidate pays before arrival. Only then do we introduce and place people against the employer's actual role and site conditions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the fastest way to test document accuracy in an interview?

Pick one entry from the career certificate and ask the candidate to describe the order of that work. A single question of that kind tests document accuracy and communication ability at the same time.

### Should candidates without experience be excluded?

No, but because E-7 is reviewed by occupation against qualifications and experience, settle first which requirement the application would rest on. Sub-types and base requirements are set out in the [E-7 specified activities guide](/en/visa/e-7), and the current year's notice from the competent authority should be checked again before applying.
