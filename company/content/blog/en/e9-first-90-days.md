## Put legal deadlines and training goals on different lines

The EPS guidance includes employment training and foreigner registration within 90 days of entry. Ninety days is a registration deadline, not permission to postpone every task until the last week. Check the current procedure and the worker's actual arrival date.

## Assign an owner to each task

| When | Employer's preparation |
| --- | --- |
| Before arrival | Confirm handover, accommodation and the employment contract |
| At handover | Check training completion and the next official steps |
| First week | Explain work, safety, pay records and contact points |
| First month | Review understanding, accommodation and attendance |
| Before registration deadline | Confirm the application and retain the relevant record |

1. Enter the arrival date in a shared deadline tracker.
2. Ask the responsible institution about documents and appointments early.
3. Record the owner and completion evidence for each item.
4. Check reporting requirements whenever employment circumstances change.

## Is a company orientation enough?

It complements the official procedure; it does not replace required training or registration. Use language the employee understands and check understanding through explanation or demonstration. Review difficulties privately with the employee and document the agreed next step.
