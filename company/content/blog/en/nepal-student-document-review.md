## Begin with the receiving institution's current checklist

Requirements differ by school, programme and intake. Use the current admission notice as the working checklist, then check embassy requirements separately. A local partner may organize documents, while the university makes its own admission decision.

## Keep a document register

| Record | What to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Name spelling and date of birth across documents |
| Academic history | Issuer, programme, completion date and required evidence |
| Language | Accepted test or course evidence for the chosen intake |
| Funding | Required format, issuer and validity conditions |
| Translation | Required language, authentication and responsible provider |

1. Obtain consent and request only necessary documents.
2. Record received, missing and clarification-needed items separately.
3. Resolve mismatches through the applicant and legitimate issuing channels.
4. Send the institution a clear list of remaining questions.

## Should a partner “fix” inconsistent records?

Never alter an original record or create substitute evidence. Request clarification or a correctly issued replacement. Limit access to sensitive documents and agree retention rules. A complete file helps review, but does not guarantee admission or a visa.
