## Prepare the team as well as the employee

Confirm who meets the employee, who explains the shift schedule and who handles questions after hours. This example is a workplace training plan; it does not replace required SSW support or safety training.

## Build the month around observed tasks

| Period | Focus | Check before moving on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Workplace tour, rules and contacts | Employee can find help |
| Days 4–7 | Observe the assigned department | Employee explains the service sequence |
| Week 2 | Practise selected tasks with supervision | Supervisor records errors and corrections |
| Week 3 | Practise handovers and unusual requests | Employee escalates appropriately |
| Week 4 | Review performance and next goals | Manager and employee agree on support |

1. Provide a short glossary for the assigned role.
2. Demonstrate one task, then ask the employee to repeat it.
3. Keep brief shift notes so feedback is consistent.
4. Check whether housing or commuting difficulties affect attendance.

## Should everybody follow the same pace?

Use common standards but adjust training time. Do not assign independent duties simply because a week has passed. Tell the employee which tasks still need a supervisor and when they will be reviewed again.
