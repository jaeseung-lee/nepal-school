## Make the support arrangement visible

SSW No. 1 employers must prepare and implement a support plan; implementation can be entrusted to a registered support organization. Registration alone does not tell you how a provider will serve your workers or communicate with your workplace.

## Ask for specific deliverables

| Area | Question to settle in writing |
| --- | --- |
| Coverage | Which tasks are covered, by whom and in which language? |
| Availability | How are routine questions and urgent issues handled? |
| Records | What reports will the employer receive and when? |
| Fees | What is included, additional or payable on termination? |
| Handover | How will records and outstanding issues be transferred? |

1. Check the provider against the current official register.
2. Compare the proposal with the support plan for the actual worker.
3. Ask to see a blank reporting template, without personal data.
4. Agree escalation contacts and review meetings.

## Does outsourcing remove the employer's responsibilities?

No. Employment management and the employer's applicable obligations still need named owners. Confirm the requirements for full versus partial outsourcing before choosing an arrangement. Keep recruitment fees and support services distinguishable in the proposal.
