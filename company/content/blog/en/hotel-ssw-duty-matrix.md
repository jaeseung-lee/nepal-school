## Write down a real shift

Official accommodation guidance describes front desk, planning and publicity, guest service, and restaurant service activities. Compare your actual vacancy with the SSW No. 1 guidance. The job title “hotel staff” alone is not enough to establish the scope of work.

## Prepare a duty matrix

| Duty | Details to agree |
| --- | --- |
| Front desk | Check-in process, reservation system and supervisor |
| Guest service | Information the employee can provide and escalation route |
| Restaurant service | Expected tasks and coordination with kitchen staff |
| Related duties | Relationship to the main work and time allocation |

1. Have the department manager list normal and peak-period tasks.
2. Identify any proposed cleaning-only or unrelated assignment for separate review.
3. Match the job description, contract and candidate briefing.
4. Recheck the arrangement before moving the person to another department.

## Does working inside a hotel make every duty eligible?

No. Check both the establishment and the actual activities. A restaurant role may raise a different field question, so verify the appropriate route rather than relying on the building's name. The table is a recruitment tool, not an official eligibility decision.
