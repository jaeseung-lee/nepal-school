## Check each requirement separately

For the usual SSW care examination route, check the care skills assessment, care Japanese assessment, and general Japanese requirement (JFT-Basic or JLPT N4 or above). A language-school certificate does not replace these results. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare lists exemption routes, including satisfactory completion of care Technical Intern Training No. 2; the supporting conditions must also be checked.

## Build a candidate evidence sheet

| Item | Record to keep |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Name and birth date matched to passport |
| Skills | Test name, result and verification method |
| Japanese | General and care-specific results recorded separately |
| Exemption | Exact route, supporting documents and unresolved conditions |

1. Ask for complete results rather than a cropped screenshot.
2. Match names across documents and clarify spelling differences.
3. Check the issuing body's current verification procedure.
4. Mark missing evidence as pending and assign a person to follow up.

Keep access to candidate documents limited to staff who need them.

## Does passing mean the candidate can start work?

No. Employment conditions, the receiving organization and immigration procedures still require review. Overseas training completion also does not establish an exemption by itself. Use the evidence sheet to decide what to verify next, not to promise a visa or start date.
