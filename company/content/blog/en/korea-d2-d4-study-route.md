## Choose the course before the visa label

Study in Korea distinguishes degree study under D-2 from non-degree training under D-4. For university Korean-language training, check the relevant D-4-1 guidance. Admission and visa issuance are separate decisions; neither should be promised by a recruitment partner.

## Compare the proposed programmes

| Question | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Academic goal | Degree programme or language preparation? |
| Entry conditions | Current school admission criteria and language requirements |
| Budget | Tuition, accommodation and living costs for the actual period |
| Documents | School requirements and separate embassy instructions |
| Next stage | Conditions for applying to a degree programme later |

1. Obtain the school's current intake notice.
2. Confirm which documents need translation or authentication.
3. Prepare a realistic funding and study plan.
4. Follow the relevant embassy's application guidance after admission.

## Does language-course completion automatically lead to a degree?

No. A subsequent degree application has its own admission and immigration requirements. Do not budget on assumed part-time earnings: permission and conditions must be checked separately. Compare programmes using their published requirements, not a promise of an easy visa.
