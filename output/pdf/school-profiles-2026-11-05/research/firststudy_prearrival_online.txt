PRE-ARRIV AL ONLINE JAPANESE CLASS  
‘Prepare Before You Arrive in Japan’ 
 
What is pre-arrival online Japanese class? 
This is an online class for students applying to FS. It helps them become familiar with native Japanese 
speakers and reach N4  level Japanese. If students enter N4, they can achieve a higher level, such as 
N2, by graduation, which is important for further studies and finding employment. 
Course details 
Start date December 2026~March 2027 
Schedule Will decide on later date 
Time Nepal time/ Japan time 
Class mode Teams/Discord 
 
Course Objective 
 Learn basic Japanese language skills (N5 level) 
 Prepare for study and life in Japan  
 Achieve N2 level or above by graduation from FS 
 
Benefits 
 Easy adjustment to school and life in Japan 
 Guidance from experienced and native teachers 
 Easier to get a part-time job after arriving in Japan 
 
Requirements 
 Discord account 
 Smartphone/laptop/Headphone 
 Stable internet connection 
 Notebooks and writing materials
