入学資格Entrance qualification

Those who have completed the 12-year course or above and have an equivalent level of education. Have Japanese proficiency equivalent to N5 of the Japanese Language Proficiency Test

入学の流れAdmission flow

  1. STEP1Submission

    After selecting your desired course, please submit your application within the deadline.

    入学願書・履歴書 / Application form

  2. STEP2Immigration procedures

    After an examination by the Immigration Bureau of the Legal Affairs Bureau, our school will issue a “Certificate of Eligibility”.

  3. STEP3Tuition payment

    Pay tuition and other fees required for admission.

  4. STEP4Issuance of certificate

    Our school will issue a “Certificate of Eligibility for Resident Status” to the enrolled students.

  5. STEP5Visa acquisition

    Apply for acquisition at a Japanese embassy or consulate abroad.

  6. STEP6Admission

    After the visa is issued, you will be admitted to Osaka International Academy.
